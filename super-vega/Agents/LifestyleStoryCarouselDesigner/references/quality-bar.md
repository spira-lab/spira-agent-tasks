# The quality bar

What a finished carousel must match. Learned from Ryan's carousels (the slides are in `style/`) and the reviewer's rejections.

## Hard rules
1. **Pain first, product last.** Pain carries slide after slide. The product appears only in the last 2–3 slides, as the fix.
2. **Show the product, don't describe it.** The payoff slide shows the real product UI on a phone or laptop: sharp, readable, real-looking data (part numbers, prices, "in stock" tags). Never AI-drawn UI. No UI (a service or a topic)? The payoff shows the result instead.
3. **A photoreal phone-camera look:** natural light, slight grain, real clutter from the audience's world. Never staged or glossy.
4. **One hero across the whole carousel:** same face, hair and outfit on every slide. Side characters (max 2 slides) carry the conflict.
4b. **People and no people mixed:** about half hero-face slides, the rest hands, first-person and objects. At least 1 no-person slide.
5. **One place per carousel**, lit consistently. Then-vs-now: one look per era.
6. **Insider copy.** The audience's own jargon, and a concrete number in every pain line. Story details ("40 scents", "3 hours") can be made up if they're plausible; numbers about the product, the market or the news must be sourced.
7. **Short text:** about 10 words per slide, 13 max, plus an optional smaller second line (SKILL.md step 4, rule 1).
8. **The last slide:** feed = proof and CTA, stats and claims from the client's brief only. Story = the win in the hero's voice; a CTA line only if the brief gives one.

## Two text styles
- **Story (9:16, 1152×2048, usually 6 slides):** white lowercase text on solid black rounded boxes (TikTok classic caption). Casual voice ("pov:", "we're so back", an emoji). One caption per slide, in open space. Time stamps can drive the story ("monday 9:02am", "friday:").
- **Feed (4:5, 1080×1350, usually 7 slides):** bold white sans with a **heavy** black outline plus a soft shadow (a thin outline vanishes on bright windows). Sentence case. The hook ends in an emoji. Optional small second line. Then-vs-now adds two coloured year pills plus a dark title pill on slide 1, in the brand colours.
- **Brand:** pills, the CTA block and any end card use the client's colors from BRIEF.md (Brand look). The product UI keeps its real colors.

## Story shapes (a ledger idea names one)
- **POV:** hook → setup → conflict → product → payoff → win.
- **Day-by-day:** "monday 9:02am" pain → manual pain → product → reaction → "thursday" → "friday: win".
- **Things only X understand / lists** (red flags, myths, mistakes, checklist, tier list, junior-me): title → one pain per slide → "Or… just ask <product>." with proof.
- **Then vs now:** each slide is two stacked photos with a thin divider (top then, bottom now), with the same activity paired. "Then" is warm, grainy, stressed; "now" is clean daylight, calm. The last slide is one full photo with the CTA block centred.
- **Text thread / mom-explain / confession / guess / countdown:** a person-to-person moment or a challenge carries the pain; the product resolves it on the last 1–2 slides.

## What reviewers rejected (strongest taste signal)
- **Text parked in one fixed top band on every slide.** Place text per image, in that photo's own negative space (beside the face, mid-frame, one side). Break long lines into narrow left-aligned stacks.
- **Paired captions split apart.** Then/now captions sit on either side of the divider and read as a pair.
- **CTA block at the top.** Centre it.
- A slide whose picture didn't sell the pain got swapped for a clearer object (a magnifier lamp over a hand-drawn footprint).

## File naming
`<Name> - <Client> - <Title> - Slide <NN> - <Maker> - v<N>.png`, where `<Name>` is the number the caller passes (e.g. `NO.035`); none → leave it off. `<Maker>` is the name the caller passes; none → `Claude`. Old versions go to `Don't use/`.
