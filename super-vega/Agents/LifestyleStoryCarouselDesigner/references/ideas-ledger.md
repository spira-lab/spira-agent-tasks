# Lifestyle story carousel ideas: the generic ledger

100+ idea templates for any brand (runs add new ones from research: G101 onward). The brief fills the slots: `<audience>` (who it's for), `<task>` (the job they do), `<pain>` (what goes wrong), `<old way>` (how they do it now), `<product>`, `<win>` (the result), `<number>`, `<time>`.
Each client keeps its own used-log (`clients/<client>/used-ideas.md`), so an idea never repeats for that client. Another client may use it.

Columns: **kind** (story shape) · **hook** (template) · **persona** (person type, made specific per brief) · **pain type** · **arc** (emotional arc across the slides) · **setting** · **format** (default; the request can override).
