# The target look: Ryan's carousels

These 26 slides are the style every carousel must match or beat. Open them before writing the copy (step 4)
and again at the self-grade (step 9). They're PartGenie posts, but the look applies to every client:
only the brand colors, the audience's world and the product UI change.

## What they do (copy this)
- **Slide count:** story 6, feed 7. That's the norm; change it only when the story needs it.
- **People and no people, mixed.** Across the 4 carousels: about half the slides show the hero's face; the rest
  are hands or first-person (holding a board, a phone, a bag of parts), objects with no person (a BOM printout,
  an open parcel, a desk at night), and a side character for the conflict (a stall owner, a cofounder, a boss).
- **No angle twice in a row:** wide establishing → close hands → side character → POV phone → object → reaction.
- **The product only at the end,** usually a phone in hand (POV) or a phone standing on the desk, with the real UI sharp.
- **Real clutter from the audience's world:** multimeters, reels, sticky notes, coffee, a second monitor.
- **Light tells the time:** blue hour, warm desk lamp, overcast day. Then-vs-now: warm grainy "then", clean daylight "now".
- **Text** sits in each photo's own empty space, never a fixed band. Story: lowercase on black boxes. Feed: bold white,
  heavy outline. Brand color only in pills and the CTA (the teal year pill in then-vs-now).
