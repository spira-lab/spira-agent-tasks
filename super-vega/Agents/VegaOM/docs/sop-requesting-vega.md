# How to Send a Request to Vega

Vega is Super Vega's operations manager. He takes the request as the task brief and runs the AI team: Echo (scripts), Atlas and the designers (talking head, explainer, podcast, avatar, clone, re-skit, corporate carousel and lifestyle story carousel), Hermes (uploads), Iris (reports) and Quill (brand briefs). This SOP shows how to ask so a request runs the first time.

## 1. Where to ask

- Write the request as the task brief of the agent job. Vega reads it and starts.
- One request per job. Vega reports progress as each stage and each video completes, and the final result lists every video with its status, credits and public URL.
- Vega does not use Lark: nothing is posted in a group or a thread, and nobody is tagged.

## 2. What to include

| Include | Examples | If you leave it out |
|---|---|---|
| **Brand or product** | Cookiy Earn Text Agent, IASO, PartGenie | Vega asks |
| **How many** | 5 videos | Vega asks |
| **Video style** | talking head, explainer, podcast, avatar image, clone, re-skit (see 2b), corporate carousel or lifestyle story carousel (see 2c) | Vega uses the brand's usual style, or asks |
| **Organic or ad** | organic | The brand's usual; otherwise Vega asks |
| **Duration** (optional) | 30 seconds | The writing style's default length |
| **Language** (optional) | Thai only | IASO rotates its 5 languages automatically |
| **Your own script** (optional) | paste it in the message | Echo writes a fresh script |
| **Special instructions** (optional) | "the bag stays visible the whole time" | None |
| **Review first?** (optional) | "don't upload yet, I'll review" | Vega waits for your word before uploading |

Brand rules already on file (voices, logos, style sheets, languages, upload tags) are applied automatically, so you don't need to repeat them.

**Examples**
- `Let's create 3 videos for Cookiy Earn Text Agent and 15 videos for IASO`
- `1 organic talking-head video for PartGenie. Don't upload yet, I'll review.`
- `Create 5 avatar images that look like typical Instagram content creators` (with no description, Vega picks the people himself)

## 2b. Cloning or re-skitting a video

Two agents work from a video you send. Say which one you want, or describe it and Vega picks:

| | 🧬 **Clone** | 🎭 **Re-skit** |
|---|---|---|
| What you get | The video's format rebuilt for the brand: new lines, voice, persona and shots | The same skit or trend, with its motion and voices kept (pitch-adjusted), the person recast with our persona, and the meaning changed to the brand |
| Good for | ads, product demos, talking heads | trending skits, reactions, memes |
| Say | `Clone this for Cookiy Earn: <link>` | `Re-skit this trend for PartGenie: <link>` |

**Required**
- **The source video:** a public link (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X) or a video file attached to the request.
- **The brand:** it must be on Vega's client list, with its DNA. A new brand gets onboarded first.
- **Clone or re-skit**, or enough description for Vega to tell. If it isn't clear, Vega asks one question.
- **How many**, if more than one.

**Optional (the agent decides when you leave it out)**
- **Persona:** name the brand's avatar or attach one. Otherwise the agent uses the brand's saved persona, or makes a new one.
- **Product images or app screenshots:** attach them if you have them. Otherwise the agent captures them from the brand's website, or designs believable ones. What you provide always wins.
- **Language or market**, and **what to change or keep** (e.g. "keep the punchline, swap the product").
- **Music:** the agent decides. Vocals only when the video needs them.
- **Organic or ad** (organic by default) and **"review first"**.

**Good to know**
- No approval step: the agent runs straight through, and the credits used are shown when the video is done. Expect about 100–150 credits per video.
- The source creator's face is never used: the person is always recast.
- Clone and re-skit videos run like any other video: up to 8 in design and 2 rendering at once.

## 2c. Carousels

The 🖼️ **Corporate Carousel Designer** makes 4–7 slide corporate carousels (1080×1350, 4:5, for Instagram and LinkedIn) in the brand's own look, plus ready-to-use captions. "Carousels" means corporate carousels unless you ask for the lifestyle kind (below).

- **Say:** `Make 3 carousels for PartGenie`
- **Required:** the brand (or product), which must be on Vega's client list with its website, and how many.
- **Optional:** a topic or angle you want. Otherwise Echo writes a fresh angle for each carousel, and a brand's carousel angles don't repeat within its last 75 carousels.
- **You get:** the contact sheet, every slide, and the captions (short-form and LinkedIn).

**Lifestyle story carousels** (new, 2026-11-03). The 📸 **Lifestyle Story Carousel Designer** makes photoreal story carousels like Ryan's: one person and one place across 5–10 slides, real-looking phone photos, the product's real screens at the end, and the captions.
- **Say:** `Make 3 lifestyle story carousels for PartGenie` (or "photo carousels", "people carousels", "story carousels like Ryan's"). Without one of these words, Vega makes corporate carousels.
- **Required:** the brand (or product), on Vega's client list with its website, and how many.
- **Optional:** a topic or pain you want, `story` (9:16) or `feed` (4:5, the default), a slide count. Otherwise the designer picks a fresh idea for each carousel from the brand's idea bank and a 100-idea list, and never repeats one for that brand.
- **Good to know:** no approval step and no Echo step. About 80–100 credits per carousel; a carousel that would cost more than 150 credits stops and Vega asks you first.
- **You get:** the same as above (contact sheet, every slide, captions), plus any facts a person should check before posting.

## 3. What happens next

1. **Vega confirms** in his first progress message what is being made.
2. **Echo writes the scripts**, checked against everything the brand already has, so angles don't repeat.
3. **Atlas produces** the videos: up to 8 in design, 2 rendering and 4 transcribing at once. New batches start in free slots as the current one finishes.
4. **Each video is reported** the moment it's done, one by one, with a short progress line (for example "IASO #146 (Task 248) done · Vietnamese · dengue protection").
5. **At the end**, Vega submits the result: one line per video with its status and the credits spent, and the list of videos with their public URLs.

Every video is delivered as its public URL, in full quality.

## 4. Reviewing and changing a video

- Send a follow-up with what to change, as specifically as you can (e.g. "make the logo smaller and embossed").
- **Fix first:** the video's own designer changes only that part and re-renders, which costs far less than a redo. Vega offers a redo or cancel only when a fix can't work.
- **The requester decides** fixes, redo or cancel for their own batch.
- Some edits depend on outside AI services. If one is down, Vega says so, along with the credits used so far and what the change would cost.

## 5. Uploading

- Say **"upload them"** in the request or a follow-up, and name any extra Task IDs to include.
- Hermes uploads to Spira under the brand's saved tag (for example **Cookiy / US**, **PartGenie / US - Video**, or each IASO country: Vietnam, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia). It only uploads; it never posts or schedules.
- To use a different tag, name it (e.g. "upload under PartGenie / US - Video"). Vega can save it as the brand's default if you ask.
- Jekylle and Angelica can decide uploads and upload tags. For Cookiy uploads, the result says when the upload is finished.

## 7. Good to know

- **Weekly targets** run Monday to Friday: Cookiy 42, IASO 100, PartGenie 50 (10 a day).
- If Vega is **blocked** (a service down, a missing tag, a decision only the owner can make), he says so in a progress message and in the result.
- During an announced **update window**, hold new requests. Anything sent during the window is picked up right after.
