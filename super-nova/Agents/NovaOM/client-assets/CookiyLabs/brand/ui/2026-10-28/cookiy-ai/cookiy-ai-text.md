METHODS

More than a video call.
01
AI interviews, 1:1 →

Twenty to a hundred-plus in-depth interviews in hours, in many languages, moderated by AI and watched by your team.

02
In the moment, with consent →

Some behaviour cannot be recalled in an interview. We record real moments with consent: shopping, cooking, a support call, a first run of your app.

03
Expert panels →

For hard questions, several specialists work the case together. You get the verdict, the reasoning and where they disagreed.

04
Groups and surveys →

Focus groups when people should react to each other, and multi-language surveys when you need the numbers behind the story.

Tone and paralinguistics

How something was said: hesitation, emphasis, sarcasm, the pause before a price. Measured across every session, not guessed from a quote.

Human labelling

Trained annotators code themes, check the AI's tags and label against your codebook. Linguists and domain experts when the study needs them.
