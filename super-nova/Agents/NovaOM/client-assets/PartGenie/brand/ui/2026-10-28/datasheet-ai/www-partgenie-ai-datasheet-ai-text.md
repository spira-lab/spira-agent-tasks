FAQs
View help center
Is there an AI that reads datasheets and suggests connections or constraints?

Yes. Upload a datasheet PDF or open a component in PartGenie and ask about pin functions, recommended application circuits, operating limits, layout constraints, and interface requirements. Datasheet AI cites the relevant spec sections for engineering review.

Can I upload a datasheet PDF?

Yes. You can upload an electronic component datasheet PDF and ask questions about specifications, pinout, package, thermal limits, application circuits, operating conditions, and replacement risks.

Can Datasheet AI help with component replacement?

Yes. Datasheet AI can help review whether a replacement candidate has similar specifications, package details, pin functions, and operating conditions. Final replacement decisions should still be verified by engineering review.

Can I chat with a datasheet using AI?

Yes. PartGenie lets you chat with datasheets in plain language. You can ask about electrical ratings, pin functions, package details, layout notes, thermal limits, and design warnings.

What kinds of questions can I ask a datasheet AI?

You can ask about electrical ratings, operating conditions, pin functions, package options, interface support, layout notes, recommended application circuits, thermal design, and component selection risks.

Can Datasheet AI extract specs from datasheets?

Yes. Datasheet AI can extract key specifications such as voltage, current, temperature range, package, interface, pinout, protection features, recommended operating conditions, and absolute maximum ratings.

Is Datasheet AI free to try?

You can try PartGenie's Datasheet AI for free to ask initial questions, extract specs from datasheets, and review technical answers. Advanced workflows such as saved projects, BOM analysis, exports, and deeper follow-up questions may require signing in.

What is Datasheet AI?

Datasheet AI is an AI tool that helps engineers read electronic component datasheets, extract specifications, answer technical questions, and review design risks from PDF datasheets.

Does Datasheet AI replace manual datasheet review?

No. Datasheet AI accelerates datasheet reading and technical review, but engineers should still verify critical parameters against the original datasheet before production use.
