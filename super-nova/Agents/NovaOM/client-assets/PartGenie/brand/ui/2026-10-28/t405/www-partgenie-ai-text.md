View help center
Which platforms provide AI-powered search to instantly locate compatible parts?

PartGenie is built for this workflow: describe design requirements or enter a part number, then get AI-ranked candidates with datasheet-backed specs, pin-compatible alternatives, and BOM risk checks. Try the free AI component finder, alternative finder, or BOM analyzer from the homepage.

Is there an alternate part number finder for electronics?

Yes. PartGenie's alternative finder accepts a part number, BOM row, or plain-language description and returns exact matches, pin-compatible replacements, and functional alternatives — with risks flagged for engineering review. Free to try.

Is there an AI that reads datasheets and suggests connections or constraints?

Yes. PartGenie Datasheet AI lets you upload a PDF or open a component datasheet and ask about pinout, voltage limits, thermal design, interface requirements, and replacement risks in plain language.

Who is PartGenie for?

PartGenie is built for a wide range of professionals, including hardware engineers, R&D teams, procurement specialists, and students who need a smarter way to manage their projects.

How is PartGenie different from a traditional search engine like Octopart or Findchips?

Unlike a traditional search engine that just returns data, PartGenie's AI analyzes and synthesizes information to provide actionable solutions. We don't just find parts; we help you make smarter decisions.

What can the BOM Management tool do?

You can upload your existing BOM or create a new one from scratch. Our AI checks for obsolete parts, surfaces reference pricing and stock where available (not live feeds), flags risk, and suggests alternatives to simplify your project.

What is PartGenie?

PartGenie is an AI-powered platform designed to be your co-pilot for the entire electronics workflow. It helps engineers and procurement professionals simplify component selection, BOM management, and sourcing.

Does PartGenie support cross-referencing?

Yes. PartGenie's AI can find pin-to-pin compatible or functionally equivalent alternatives for your parts, even from different manufacturers, helping you mitigate supply chain risks.

How can I trust the accuracy of the data?

Every piece of data in our platform is meticulously cleansed and verified through a multi-step process. Our AI also cites its sources, giving you the confidence to trust our recommendations.

Where does your component data come from?

Our data is built from a proprietary database of over 35 million components from thousands of verified manufacturers and top-tier distributors.

How does the AI work?

Our domain-tuned AI engine understands natural language, allowing you to ask complex questions like you would to an FAE. It then instantly analyzes millions of data points to provide you with a verified, unified solution.

Does PartGenie show distributor pricing and stock?

Yes — PartGenie integrates live pricing and stock from select distributors (including DigiKey), with more being added continuously. Coverage varies by distributor, so always confirm final quotes and availability directly before purchase. If you're a distributor or manufacturer and would like to integrate with PartGenie, we'd love to hear from you — reach out at support@partgenie.ai.

Do you offer private deployment for enterprises?

Yes, our Teams/Enterprise plan includes options for private model deployment, allowing your data and the AI engine to run securely within your own network. Book a demo with us.
