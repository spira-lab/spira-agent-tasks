# Nova — Client Data Sheet

Owned by Nova. All-time output totals per client. One row per brand.

## Definitions

- **Project** = 1 original output (1 original video or 1 image post).
- **Projects completed** = Original videos + Image posts.
- **Original videos** = Organic videos + Ad videos.
- **Image posts** = Organic posts + Ad posts + Carousels.
- **Repurpose videos** are counted separately and are not projects. Retired 2026-10-24: none are made any more; the column keeps what was logged before.
- **Do not count** rejected or failed outputs.
