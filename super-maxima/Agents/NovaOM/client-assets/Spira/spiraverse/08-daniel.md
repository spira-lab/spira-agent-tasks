#### Expanded bio

**Profile bio (short):** Founder. Shipping product and, finally, content. Showing how a busy team posts every day. 🚀 Comment SPIRA.

**Who he is:** Daniel is in his early thirties, Korean-American, and runs a small software startup. For a year the company's socials were "on his list", somewhere below hiring, fundraising and shipping. Now he shows how a busy founder keeps a brand posting without hiring a content team. His message: if you're building something, people should see it being built. He shows people how his company posts every day with Spira, in the time between two meetings.

**Personality and voice:** Dry, honest and self-deprecating, calm delivery with a half-smile. He switches between three registers:

- **Founder truth:** what actually happens in a small startup.
- **Self-roast:** his old posting habits, investor-update panic, his agency's pitches.
- **Builder mode:** how he set up the company's content in one afternoon.

**Lifestyle:** Runs in the morning, ships in the afternoon, answers email at night. Bad at rest days.

#### Content pillars

- **Founder truth:** the unglamorous side of a small startup.
- **The company's content engine:** the Brand DNA set once, then posts drafted, reviewed and scheduled.
- **Growth for builders:** what to post when you're building, not selling.
- **His life:** runs, coffee, late-night shipping.

#### Audience

Founders, indie hackers and small startup teams with no marketing hire.
