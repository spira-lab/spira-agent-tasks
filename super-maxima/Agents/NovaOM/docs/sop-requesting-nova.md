# How to Send a Request to Nova

Nova is Super Nova's operations manager. She takes requests in Lark and runs the AI team: Echo (scripts), Atlas and the designers (talking head, explainer, podcast, avatar, clone, re-skit, corporate carousel and lifestyle story carousel), Hermes (uploads), Iris (reports) and Quill (brand briefs). This SOP shows how to ask so a request runs the first time.

## 1. Where to ask

- In the **SuperMaxima** group, start your message with **@Nova**. Without the tag, Nova does not see it.
- One request per message. Nova replies in that message's **thread**, and everything about that request (questions, each finished video, the close) stays in that thread.
- Direct messages to Nova are for the owner only.

## 2. What to include

| Include | Examples | If you leave it out |
|---|---|---|
| **Brand or product** | Cookiy Earn Text Agent, IASO, PartGenie | Nova asks |
| **How many** | 5 videos | Nova asks |
| **Video style** | talking head, explainer, podcast, avatar image, clone, re-skit (see 2b), corporate carousel or lifestyle story carousel (see 2c) | Nova uses the brand's usual style, or asks |
| **Organic or ad** | organic | The brand's usual; otherwise Nova asks |
| **Duration** (optional) | 30 seconds | The writing style's default length |
| **Language** (optional) | Thai only | IASO rotates its 5 languages automatically |
| **Your own script** (optional) | paste it in the message | Echo writes a fresh script |
| **Special instructions** (optional) | "the bag stays visible the whole time" | None |
| **Review first?** (optional) | "don't upload yet, I'll review" | Nova waits for your word before uploading |

Brand rules already on file (voices, logos, style sheets, languages, upload tags) are applied automatically, so you don't need to repeat them.

**Examples**
- `@Nova let's create 3 videos for Cookiy Earn Text Agent and 15 videos for IASO`
- `@Nova 1 organic talking-head video for PartGenie. Don't upload yet, I'll review.`
- `@Nova create 5 avatar images that look like typical Instagram content creators` (with no description, Nova picks the people herself)

## 2b. Cloning or re-skitting a video

Two agents work from a video you send. Say which one you want, or describe it and Nova picks:

| | 🧬 **Clone** | 🎭 **Re-skit** |
|---|---|---|
| What you get | The video's format rebuilt for the brand: new lines, voice, persona and shots | The same skit or trend, with its motion and voices kept (pitch-adjusted), the person recast with our persona, and the meaning changed to the brand |
| Good for | ads, product demos, talking heads | trending skits, reactions, memes |
| Say | `@Nova clone this for Cookiy Earn: <link>` | `@Nova re-skit this trend for PartGenie: <link>` |

**Required**
- **The source video:** a public link (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X) or a video file in the message.
- **The brand:** it must be on Nova's client list, with its DNA. A new brand gets onboarded first.
- **Clone or re-skit**, or enough description for Nova to tell. If it isn't clear, Nova asks one question.
- **How many**, if more than one.

**Optional (the agent decides when you leave it out)**
- **Persona:** name the brand's avatar or attach one. Otherwise the agent uses the brand's saved persona, or makes a new one.
- **Product images or app screenshots:** attach them if you have them. Otherwise the agent captures them from the brand's website, or designs believable ones. What you provide always wins.
- **Language or market**, and **what to change or keep** (e.g. "keep the punchline, swap the product").
- **Music:** the agent decides. Vocals only when the video needs them.
- **Organic or ad** (organic by default) and **"review first"**.

**Good to know**
- No approval step: the agent runs straight through, and the credits used are shown when the video is done. Expect about 100–150 credits per video.
- The source creator's face is never used: the person is always recast.
- Clone and re-skit videos run like any other video: up to 6 in design and 2 rendering at once.

## 2c. Carousels

The 🖼️ **Corporate Carousel Designer** makes 4–7 slide corporate carousels (1080×1350, 4:5, for Instagram and LinkedIn) in the brand's own look, plus ready-to-use captions. "Carousels" means corporate carousels unless you ask for the lifestyle kind (below).

- **Say:** `@Nova make 3 carousels for PartGenie`
- **Required:** the brand (or product), which must be on Nova's client list with its website, and how many.
- **Optional:** a topic or angle you want. Otherwise Echo writes a fresh angle for each carousel, and a brand's carousel angles don't repeat within its last 75 carousels.
- **You get:** in your thread, the contact sheet, every slide, and the captions (short-form and LinkedIn).

**Lifestyle story carousels** (new, 2026-11-03). The 📸 **Lifestyle Story Carousel Designer** makes photoreal story carousels like Ryan's: one person and one place across 5–10 slides, real-looking phone photos, the product's real screens at the end, and the captions.
- **Say:** `@Nova make 3 lifestyle story carousels for PartGenie` (or "photo carousels", "people carousels", "story carousels like Ryan's"). Without one of these words, Nova makes corporate carousels.
- **Required:** the brand (or product), on Nova's client list with its website, and how many.
- **Optional:** a topic or pain you want, `story` (9:16) or `feed` (4:5, the default), a slide count. Otherwise the designer picks a fresh idea for each carousel from the brand's idea bank and a 100-idea list, and never repeats one for that brand.
- **Good to know:** no approval step and no Echo step. About 80–100 credits per carousel; a carousel that would cost more than 150 credits stops and Nova asks you first.
- **You get:** the same as above (contact sheet, every slide, captions), plus any facts a person should check before posting.

## 3. What happens next

1. **Nova confirms** in your thread what is being made.
2. **Echo writes the scripts**, checked against everything the brand already has, so angles don't repeat.
3. **Atlas produces** the videos: up to 6 in design and 2 rendering at once. New batches start in free slots as the current one finishes.
4. **Each video is posted** in your thread the moment it's done, one by one, with a short line (for example "IASO #146 (Task 248) done · Vietnamese · dengue protection").
5. **At the end**, Nova closes the batch: she tags you, gives the credits used and the weekly progress, and asks about the upload.

Videos over 30 MB arrive in Lark as a compressed preview. Nova posts the full-quality file's location.

## 4. Reviewing and changing a video

- Reply in the thread with what to change, as specifically as you can (e.g. "make the logo smaller and embossed").
- **Fix first:** the video's own designer changes only that part and re-renders, which costs far less than a redo. Nova offers a redo or cancel only when a fix can't work.
- **The requester decides** fixes, redo or cancel for their own batch.
- Some edits depend on outside AI services. If one is down, Nova says so, along with the credits used so far and what the change would cost.

## 5. Uploading

- Say **"upload them"** in the thread, and name any extra Task IDs to include.
- Hermes uploads to Spira under the brand's saved tag (for example **Cookiy / US**, **PartGenie / US - Video**, or each IASO country: Vietnam, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia). It only uploads; it never posts or schedules.
- To use a different tag, name it (e.g. "upload under PartGenie / US - Video"). Nova can save it as the brand's default if you ask.
- Jekylle and Angelica can decide uploads and upload tags. For Cookiy uploads, Nova tags Felix when they finish.

## 6. Other things you can ask

| Ask | Who can ask |
|---|---|
| `@Nova report please`: the track sheet with targets, uploads, credits and recent changes | Anyone |
| `@Nova write a script for <brand>`: scripts only, delivered as a file or a Lark doc | Anyone |
| `@Nova stop`: halts a running edit straight away | The requester or the owner |
| A new client or brand brief, targets, backups, deleting anything | The owner only |

## 7. Good to know

- **Weekly targets** run Monday to Friday: Cookiy 42, IASO 100, PartGenie 50 (10 a day).
- If Nova is **blocked** (a service down, a missing tag, a decision only the owner can make), she tells you in the thread and messages the owner.
- During an announced **update window**, hold new requests. Anything sent during the window is picked up right after.
