## Production

How the batch sheet rows for this brand are filled.

- Strategy: nobody follows a POS system, so we build food accounts people follow for the food. Every video features a real MenuSifu restaurant. (from Quill's approved brief)
- Content accounts: (1) City food explorer, a recurring local food host with a signature verdict line; (2) Behind the kitchen, close-up kitchen footage with no talking and dish names on screen; (3) Sip and sweet, drink-making ASMR with a colour-coded rating; (4) Restaurant people, a friendly host who gives each restaurant a human face; (5) Operator tips, a restaurant-industry host who speaks owner to owner. (from Quill's approved brief)
- Visual identity: each video wears the restaurant's identity, not MenuSifu's. Phone-shot feel with real reactions and real sound, never a polished commercial. Show the craving in the first second; name the dish, district and restaurant every time. Captions are lower case in a creator's voice and vary in style and placement across the set. Music from Suno. (from Quill's approved brief)
- Voice: sounds like a real creator posting, not a brand. A conflict or joke in the first second, beats timed to the action, then a payoff. Never oversells. (from Quill's approved brief)
- MenuSifu's role: only a natural cameo (kiosk, scan to order, E-Menu tablet), never a plug, and only current products shown working as they do in the restaurant. On screen: no logo tags or brand pills; the restaurant name goes inside the captions. Speech: personas only talk if the original person talks, using Wan's native voice. (from Quill's approved brief)
- Objective: show the client that a proven format can carry their real restaurant. CTA: "📍[Restaurant], if you wanna try it" plus a follow line such as "follow for my next restaurant tour" (and similar). Success signal: the client asks "Can we do this for all of them?" (from Quill's approved brief)

They search "best ___ near me", follow local food creators, and care about value, portions and the experience. Tastes: Asian and Latin cuisines, bubble tea, hot pot, BBQ, all-you-can-eat. (from Quill's approved brief)
