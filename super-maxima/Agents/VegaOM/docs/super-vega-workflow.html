Agent system · workflow

Super Vega

How a request travels from a message in Lark to a finished video, avatar or carousel on Spira, and who touches it on the way. Vega assigns, Atlas runs, designers build, Hermes uploads.

You & the team→Lark→Vega→batch sheet→Atlas→designers→Project/<Brand>/→Hermes→Spira
1 · The hand-off

One request, one shared sheet, every video back to Lark

Vega never talks to a designer and never reads a script. He hands Atlas the path to one batch sheet. Each designer messages him the moment its video is done, and he posts it to the request's Lark thread right away.

2 · Inside a batch

What happens to one row

Every video is one row in the sheet. Its status moves left to right. A problem goes back to the video's own designer to fix first; redo or cancel only when a fix can't work, and the requester decides.

3 · After a batch

From finished video to Spira

When Atlas's line comes in, Vega closes each sheet on its own and tags its requester. Everything after that runs only on someone's word.

Vega

Close and log

Tallies the sheet, logs the originals toward each brand's weekly target, totals the credits, and tags the requester in their thread.

Hermes

Upload to Spira

On the requester's word: uploads each video under its tag, such as Cookiy / US or one IASO country tag per language. Upload only, never posts.

Iris

Report

The track sheet: made vs target per brand, uploaded vs made, credits spent and their dollar value, storage, and recent changes.

Jani

Back up

On the owner's word only: moves finished projects to the external drive. Task numbers never change.

4 · Task numbers

Two numbers per video

The Task ID tracks a task across the whole system and names its files. The Brand number is each brand's own count, 01, 02, 03 with no gaps, however the brands are interleaved.

Both come from one call to the batch tool, which issues every row's numbers at once. Nobody counts files in a folder, so backing videos up never restarts the count.

Keeping the counts honest

Redo keeps both numbers. Cancel frees both, and the next task takes the lowest free number. Avatars count under their own brand, Avatar, so they never take a client's video numbers. A test run moves to Test and frees its brand number.

6 · Standing rules

How decisions get made

  • Fix before redoPropose a targeted fix by the video's own designer first. Redo or cancel only when a fix can't work.
  • The requester decidesWhoever asked for a batch decides its fixes, redo or cancel. Jekylle and Angelica may also decide uploads and upload tags.
  • Every video, the moment it's doneEach finished video goes to the request's Lark thread one by one, never held back for the end of the batch.
  • Blocked means a DMWhen Vega or any agent is blocked, the owner gets a Lark DM right away: what, why, and what is needed.
  • Spend is shared, balance is notReports show the credits the designers spent and their dollar value. The account balance stays private.
  • Every fix leaves a noteEach change to the system gets one dev note, shown under Recent changes on the report.
  • Back up before a major updateThe whole system is zipped into Backup/ and checked before the first change of any major update.
  • Credits come from each jobEvery generation records the exact charge Spira reports for it, at Spira's live prices.
  • No gates, full speedClone and re-skit run straight through, in the same slots as every other row. Slowing down to test is the team's call.
