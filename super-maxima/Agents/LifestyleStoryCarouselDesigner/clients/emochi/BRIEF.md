# Emochi: brief

Brief status: **draft**. Written 2026-11-02 from the team's demo videos (Ken's "Spira AI - Client Demo Videos"), not from a client brief. The skill verifies every line against the client's site on its first run and marks what it can't confirm `[guessed]`.

Product: an AI character chat app: roleplay and talk with AI characters.
Product type: app (phone)
Where it shows: phone in hand
Real features: thousands of characters, or create your own · realistic conversations that reply in seconds · characters send voice messages · memory (they remember your moments) · stories from thousands of creators
Audience: young women who love fictional characters, comfort characters, book boyfriends, anime and romance stories
Markets: English-speaking [guessed]
Pains, in their words: feeling lonely at night · coming home after a hard day with no one to talk to · wishing a favourite character were real
Claims allowed: chat free at emochi.com
Never say: that it replaces real relationships or therapy

## Brand look
- Colors: brand yellow #FEFC00 (CSS var --color-brand-main), near-black #111111 / #010101, light grey #F1F1F1. The demo videos showed a black end card, a yellow accent and a white mascot; the black and yellow are confirmed by the site, but the mascot was not seen there.
- Font: Geist, plus Geist Mono. The serif captions in the demo videos are TikTok styling, not a site font [guessed].
- Logo: there is an Emochi logo at top left, but it's an inline graphic with no file URL or alt text. Its look and colour are not confirmed. Favicon: https://emochi.com/favicon.ico
- Tone: casual, free and fan-community: "Immersive Roleplay with AI characters for free".
- Where it shows on a carousel: pills, CTA block and end-card in these colors; the product UI keeps its own real colors; caption text stays white-on-black (story) or white with outline (feed).
