## Brand look
- Colors: Cookiy Earn site (earn.cookiy.ai) uses ink #0F172A, yellow #F0A500 (its accent and highlight), green #1F7A4D, greys #5B6573 / #F6F7F9. The main B2B site (cookiy.ai) is a different palette (peach #FFA86B, ink #000 / #0A0A0A, cream #FCFAF7), so don't use it for Earn. The demo videos showed a white end card, an iOS-blue "Just text it ↑" pill and yellow accents on a dark header. The blue is the iOS message colour, not a Cookiy colour, and the dark header was not confirmed on the site.
- Font: Inter (Google Fonts) on Earn. The main cookiy.ai site uses Geist.
- Logo: Earn shows a "CookiyEarn" text wordmark in the header and footer, with no separate Earn logo file found. The parent logo file is https://cookiy.ai/images/cookiy-logo.svg. Its colour is not confirmed.
- Tone: Plain and reassuring, money-first: "All from home, on your schedule." · "Free to join · No membership fee, ever".
- Where it shows on a carousel: pills, CTA block and end-card in these colors; the product UI keeps its own real colors; caption text stays white-on-black (story) or white with outline (feed).
