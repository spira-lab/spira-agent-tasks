PartGenie—SocialContentBrief
1. Thebrand
PartGenieisanAIassistantforhardwareengineers.Ithelpsthemfindelectroniccomponents,
understanddatasheets,designcircuits,andbuildandsourceabillofmaterials(BOM).
Tagline:"BuildhardwarewithAIyoucanverify."Thesocialbioreads"Theintelligencelayer
forhardwaredesign.Components.Datasheets.Betterdecisions."
PartGenieisownedbyAuraLoomLLCinAnnArbor,Michigan.ItlaunchedinNovember2025.
Thefounder'sparentsrananelectronicsfactory,andthat'stheoriginoftheidea.

2. Theproduct
•
Hugepartsdatabase:56M+componentsfrom12,700+manufacturers.
•
Search:lookupparts,specsanddatasheetsinplainEnglish,findpin-compatible
alternatives,andcomparepartssidebyside.
•
Chatwithadatasheet:askanyspecquestioninsteadofreadinghundredsofpages.
•
Uploadandidentify:circuitdiagrams,PCBphotos,chipmarkings,datasheetsorBOMs.The
AIrecognisestheparts.
•
AIHardwareDesigner:describeaproductanditproposesthearchitecture,pickspartsand
buildstheBOM.
•
SmartBOM:flagsobsoleteorriskyparts,showspricingandstock,findscheaper
alternatives,andletsyouchatwithyourBOM.
•
Design-readyECAD:symbols,footprintsand3DmodelsforAltium,KiCad,Eagle,OrCAD,
Allegroandmore.
•
Sourcing:comparespricing,stock,MOQandavailabilityacrossdistributors,plusatariff
lookup.
•
Everywhereyouwork:web,iOS,AndroidandaChromeextension.
•
Keypromise:answerstracebacktomanufacturerdatasheetsandtrustedsources.It'sAIyou
canverify,unlikeChatGPT.

4. Theaudience
•
Hardwareandelectricalengineers:theydesignPCBsandpickparts.
•
FieldApplicationEngineers(FAEs):theybuildsolutionsandBOMsforcustomers.
•
Sourcingandprocurementteams:theymanagesupplyrisk,alternativesandcost.
•
Students,makersandhobbyists:theyusethefreetierandworkonArduino,ESP32and
KiCadprojects.
Theirpainpoints:hoursspentdiggingthroughdatasheets,partsthatgoobsoleteoroutof
stock,findingdrop-inreplacements,andbuildingBOMsbyhand.
